# Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) assessment

**Not legal advice — a lawyer should confirm this conclusion**, but it is
written to be checked against, not just asserted.

## What triggers SDF status

DPDP Act 2023 §10 lets the Central Government notify certain data fiduciaries
as "Significant Data Fiduciaries" based on factors including:

- **Volume and sensitivity of personal data processed**
- **Risk to electoral democracy, state security, public order**
- **Potential impact on data principals' sovereignty/integrity of India**
- Other factors the government may specify

As of this assessment, the **Central Government has not yet notified the
specific volume/sensitivity thresholds or a list of designated SDFs** — the
rules operationalizing §10 were still pending at time of writing. This means
no data fiduciary, including this clinic, is currently a *designated* SDF,
because the designation mechanism itself has not been switched on.

## Why this is still worth assessing now

Two factors specific to this practice make it a plausible future candidate
once thresholds are set, so it is worth tracking rather than ignoring:

1. **Sensitivity**: health data is explicitly called out as sensitive
   personal data (SPDI Rule 3), and paediatric health data compounds that —
   nearly every record here belongs to a child, which is its own aggravating
   factor under §9.
2. **Volume**: currently **low**. This is a single-location paediatric
   clinic with, per current staffing, one doctor and one staff member using
   the system (see clinic scale note) — the realistic patient volume is in
   the hundreds to low thousands, not the scale (typically framed around
   large platforms, telecom, or major hospital networks) that SDF thresholds
   are expected to target.

## Conclusion

**Not a Significant Data Fiduciary today, and unlikely to qualify by volume
even once thresholds are published**, given the clinic's current single-
location, low-staff scale. This should be **re-assessed if**:

- Patient volume grows substantially (multi-location expansion, a chain, or
  a large single-site practice),
- The Central Government publishes SDF thresholds/rules, or
- The business model changes to aggregate data across many clinics/practices
  (e.g. becoming a platform other clinics use), which would change the
  volume calculus significantly.

## If SDF status were triggered

For completeness, §10 obligations if ever triggered would include:

- Appointing a **Data Protection Officer based in India**, who represents
  the fiduciary for grievance/Board purposes.
- Appointing an **independent data auditor** and carrying out **periodic
  audits**.
- Conducting a **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** for high-risk
  processing.
- Undertaking such other measures as prescribed.

None of this is being built now, since the conclusion above is that it does
not currently apply — but this section exists so the checklist is not
silently dropped if the assessment is revisited later.

## Status

Assessed **not applicable** at current scale. Revisit on the triggers listed
above, or annually alongside the rest of the compliance review, whichever
comes first.
